## Full description (≤ 4000)
Vezzo is a habit app built to be quick: pick what you want to keep, set the day's goal and track your progress without the clutter.

**What you can do**
• Create habits with an emoji, a goal (glasses, minutes, pages, times or just "done") and the days of the week.
• Get reminders at the times you choose, and complete, snooze for 1 hour or skip right from the notification.
• See your streak, the weekly summary and a 4-week history for each habit.
• Make it yours with 7 accent colors and light, dark or automatic mode.
• Works on iPhone, iPad and foldables, with a two-column layout when there is room.
• Your entries are saved on the device and sync when the connection returns.

**Privacy**
No ads and no cross-app tracking. You can delete your account and data inside the app, under Profile. Privacy Policy: https://vezzo.cloud/privacy · Terms of Use: https://vezzo.cloud/terms

Available in Portuguese and English.
